VFS Stuff (Looking at XV6's source code):
sys_open():
    Checks mode (O_CREATE, O_RDONLY, etc)
    Checks type (T_DIR, T_DEVICE)
    T_DEVICE:
        'iunlockput()' --> 'unlock, then put'
        iunlock() --> Unlocks the inode (what the fuck is an inode specifically)
            An inode: Sotres all the metadata about a file, EXCEPT for the filename
            Essentially, my VFS HAS to have inode support
